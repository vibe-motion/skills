# Story Patterns

## 15-second product hypercut

Use 450 frames at 30fps:

| Frames | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| 0-36 | Authentic logo/brand hook |
| 30-105 | User intent, input, or setup |
| 96-180 | Thinking, mechanism, or feature execution |
| 168-270 | Capabilities and scenarios |
| 258-360 | Outputs, results, or proof |
| 348-405 | Product payoff |
| 393-450 | Accurate logo and CTA |

Overlap 6-12 frames when outgoing motion naturally supplies the next shot.

## 18-second UI launch

1. Logo hook
2. Input expansion and typed prompt
3. Send/commit event
4. Thinking or tool routing
5. Parallel task cards
6. Streaming result
7. Capability declaration
8. Logo lockup

Favor readable UI, tight holds, and typography-driven impacts.

## 22-second chromatic product film

1. Brand motif
2. Product input
3. Transformation into branded environment
4. Feature/scenario sequence
5. Results workspace
6. Metrics or proof
7. Color collapse into the official mark

Keep the brand environment subordinate to the product chain.

## 28-30 second premium launch

Use 3-4 major impact peaks and controlled breathing space:

1. Restrained brand establishment
2. Product interaction
3. Mechanism/thinking
4. Capability architecture
5. Results
6. Hero claim
7. Premium logo lockup

Use spatial depth and braking instead of filling every frame.

## Copy

Default to English. Use short, active phrases:

- 1-5 words for impact copy
- 6-12 words for supporting claims
- One primary message per beat

Use official terminology. Do not invent a tagline that could be mistaken for an official slogan without labeling the film as a concept.
